# Reactor 1.2.0 — Online beta

- Create an account, claim a unique username and invite friends by name.
- Play friendly duels or join ranked matchmaking across Windows and Android.
- Climb seven ranks with GPT Image badges, animated progress and protection meters.
- Earn points by winning; losses deduct points and can lower your current rank.
- Compete for the 100 Singularity seats. Higher scores replace the lowest holders; ties favor incumbents.
- Keep your lifetime highest achieved rank, highest score and win/loss statistics.
- Reconnect to online matches and return to your offline saved game safely.
- Retain the real CC0 sounds, settings, particles and hidden chain lengths.

Email verification and password-reset emails are deferred for this beta. Accounts still use email and a password of at least ten characters. Seasons are inactive: “Seasons coming soon.” Ranked turns allow two minutes; a missed turn or resignation counts as a loss. Friendly duels do not change your ranked points.

This is the direct Android and private Windows beta. Google Play publication remains paused for the owner's approval; public Windows distribution continues through the separate Microsoft Store process.

Verification includes 24 local rules/database checks, 12 live server checks, 19 native regression checks and a separate native live flow that created two accounts, completed an 82-move ranked duel and restored the offline save. Disposable QA users were removed from the live backend.

Sounds: Kenney Interface Sounds and Digital Audio, CC0, with retained source licences. Rank artwork: GPT Image, with retained prompts and masters.
