# Reactor

**Small moves. Explosive chains.** A chain-reaction strategy game made by Bennet.

Play ranked online, share an arena with friends, challenge Reactor AI, play with 2–4 people on one screen, or solve a takeover in Chain Lab.

## Download

- [Android 1.2.1 APK](https://github.com/Benighter/Reactor-Releases/releases/download/v1.2.1/Reactor-1.2.1.apk)
- [Latest release and notes](https://github.com/Benighter/Reactor-Releases/releases/latest)
- [Benighter Personal Projects](https://benighters-personal-projects.vercel.app/releases/reactor/)

Android 8.0 or newer and an ARM64 device are required. This direct beta is distributed outside Google Play. Future releases appear inside **Settings → More → Updates**; Android approval is required to install them.

The Windows 1.2.1 beta is installed locally and backed up privately. Public Windows distribution continues through the separate Microsoft Store process. Google Play publication remains paused until the owner approves the beta.

## Version 1.2.1 — Play together

- Fair ranked matching with bounded score and rank gaps. PC searches continue while the window is unfocused.
- Live online usernames, player activity and searching counts.
- Friends by username, profiles with earned achievements, and invitations into playable shared arenas.
- Unique-username availability, matching passwords, Show/Hide and identity-preserving username changes.
- Working password recovery through a private, one-use email link.
- Seven animated ranks, lower-rank protection, lifetime peaks and a dynamic top 100.
- Real CC0 sounds, vibrant effects and offline saved games are retained.

Email confirmation stays off during this beta. Passwords require at least ten characters. Seasons are inactive: “Seasons coming soon.” Ranked turns allow two minutes; missed turns and resignations count as losses. Friendly duels do not change ranked points.
