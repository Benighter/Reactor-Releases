# Reactor: The Ripple Effect

**Small moves. Explosive chains.** Place one atom, set off a chain and turn neighbouring cells into your territory.

Learn in ten playable lessons, play solo against Reactor AI or share one phone for a local match. Online play includes private rooms and ranked duels, teams and free-for-all matches.

## Version 1.3.3 — A new first impression

- Four clear mode cards, contextual play actions and quick setup, help and settings together.
- Touch/mouse-reactive atom art, animated selection and keyboard focus feedback, with reduced-motion support.
- Compact offline results and a cleaner online rank recap with clear rematch readiness.

- Rematches after every original player accepts; same players, teams, format and clock. Declined offers keep your choices for new rivals.
- Animated saved ranked points and cinematic GPT rank badge promotions, with accurate losses, deranking and protection.
- Fixed damaged online punctuation. Placements animate immediately and reconcile with the server; rejected moves recover safely.
- Faster active-match checks, adaptive opponent polling and shorter bot thinking time.

- Three clearly disclosed rated bot introductions per format for new Spark players. Wins and losses affect rank, with normal protection. Skip to real players at any time before starting. Starts are capped permanently on the server; bots never occupy leaderboard places.
- Ten skippable hands-on lessons, replayable from How to Play.
- 1v1, 2v2 teams, three-player and four-player free-for-all.
- Independent ranks, protection, lifetime peaks and dynamic top-100 boards for every format. Existing 1v1 progress carries forward.
- Friendly rooms with exact username invitations, seat acceptance and a host ready gate. Teammates share cells and wins.
- Spark 1+1, Surge 3+0/3+1 and Orbit 10+0/10+1 total time banks. Every increment is exactly one second.
- Server-owned online time, per-seat timeout and idempotent move retries. Ranked searches match the same format and clock within a bounded skill gap.
- Offline Solo clocks pause and save. Existing untimed saves remain untimed.
- Friends, earned achievements, live player lists, unique usernames and password recovery carry forward.
- Real CC0 sounds, vibrant effects and offline saves are retained.

Email confirmation is off for the beta. Passwords require ten characters. Seasons are inactive: **Seasons coming soon**. Friendly rooms do not change ranked points.
