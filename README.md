# Reactor

**Small moves. Explosive chains.** A chain-reaction strategy game made by Bennet.

Play ranked online, invite friends by unique username, challenge Reactor AI, share one screen with 2–4 players, or solve a one-move takeover in Chain Lab.

## Download

- [Android 1.2.0 APK](https://github.com/Benighter/Reactor-Releases/releases/download/v1.2.0/Reactor-1.2.0.apk)
- [Latest release and notes](https://github.com/Benighter/Reactor-Releases/releases/latest)
- [Benighter Personal Projects](https://benighters-personal-projects.vercel.app/releases/reactor/)

Android 8.0 or newer and an ARM64 device are required. This is a direct test release, distributed outside Google Play. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser. Future releases can be offered inside **Settings → More → Updates**, with Android approval required to install them.

The Windows 1.2.0 beta is installed locally and backed up privately. Public Windows distribution continues through the separate Microsoft Store process. Google Play publication is paused until this beta is approved.

## Version 1.2.0 — Online beta

- Create an account, claim a unique username and invite friends by name.
- Friendly duels and ranked matchmaking across Android and Windows.
- Seven ranks, animated progress, lower-rank protection and 100 Singularity seats.
- Lifetime highest rank, highest score and win/loss statistics.
- Real CC0 sounds, offline saved games and vibrant effects are retained.

Email confirmation and password-reset emails are deferred for this beta. Sign in immediately with email and a password of at least ten characters. Seasons are inactive: “Seasons coming soon.” Ranked turns allow two minutes; a missed turn or resignation counts as a loss. Friendly duels do not change ranked points.
