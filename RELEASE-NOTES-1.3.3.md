# Reactor 1.3.3 — A new first impression

Find your next game in four clear mode cards. The play button follows your choice, and the glowing atom scene reacts to your touch or mouse.

- A cleaner, responsive main menu for Android and Windows, with simple mode explanations, quick setup, Learn to Play and Settings together.
- Animated selection, hover, press and keyboard focus feedback. Tapping the atom scene creates a ripple; reduced motion keeps the final states easy to read.
- Compact offline match recaps with clear moves and biggest-chain statistics.
- A polished online result screen puts the outcome, earned or lost points, next-rank progress and rematch actions together. Rank promotions retain their cinematic badge transition.
- Clear individual readiness for two-, three- and four-player rematches. Everyone must accept; a declined offer keeps the same format and clock for finding new rivals.
- Existing accounts, ranked rules, timers, friends, ten playable lessons, saved games and real CC0 sounds carry forward.

Validation: 23 distinct native checks passed, including real winning chains and audio, all four menu choices, settings and reduced motion, ranked promotion/deranking, rematch readiness and save isolation. Real Unity layouts were reviewed at desktop and phone dimensions. The Windows installation matches all 160 build files and retains the owner's saved preferences and encrypted session. Android's in-place installation reported success with the same signing identity; the phone disconnected before its final physical screen check.

The direct Android beta remains free, with zero ads and no trial timer or checkout. Android asks you to approve installation. The private Windows copy is updated locally. This release makes no change to the separate Google Play or Microsoft Store submissions, pricing, trial or payment setup.
