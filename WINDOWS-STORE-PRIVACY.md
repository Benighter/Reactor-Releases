# Reactor Windows Store edition privacy notice

Publisher: Benighter. This notice applies to the Windows Microsoft Store edition of **Reactor: The Ripple Effect**. The Store release is being prepared; this notice does not announce its availability.

Reactor saves match progress and game settings on your device. The game does not require an account and does not include advertising or developer analytics. Gameplay is available offline. It does not collect or upload your saved matches, contacts or location.

Microsoft Store handles acquisition and updates for this edition. Choosing Updates opens Microsoft Store. Microsoft may process information under its own [privacy policy](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement) when you use its services.

If you contact support through GitHub, your report and any information you choose to include are handled by GitHub under its [privacy policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Avoid posting personal information or saved files you do not want to share publicly. [Contact Reactor support](https://github.com/Benighter/Reactor-Releases/issues).

The directly distributed Android and classic Windows editions use a separate release-update service. This notice covers the Windows Microsoft Store edition only.
