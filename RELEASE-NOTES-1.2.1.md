# Reactor 1.2.1 — Play together

- Ranked searches keep working when the PC window loses focus. Searches pair nearby scores and ranks; the range widens gradually to a firm limit, so new players are never matched against the highest ranks simply because few people are online.
- See online players, their usernames and activity, plus how many players are searching.
- Find friends by username, accept requests, view earned achievements and invite friends into a playable shared arena. Friendly games keep ranked points unchanged.
- Choose a unique username during signup, check availability, confirm your password and show or hide it. Existing players can change their username while keeping friends and progress.
- Forgot password now delivers a private, one-use email link and opens Reactor's password recovery page.
- Seven ranks, rank protection, lifetime peaks, the dynamic top 100, offline saves and real CC0 sounds carry forward. Seasons are coming soon.

Android updates through Reactor's existing in-app update channel. Its package and signing identity are unchanged. Windows uses the existing installed location and keeps local saves. Google Play publication remains paused for owner testing; the classic Windows installer remains private while the separate Microsoft Store process continues.
