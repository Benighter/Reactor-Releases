# Reactor 1.3.1 — A spark to start with

New Spark players can choose three rated bot introductions in each online format: 1v1, 2v2 teams, three-player free-for-all and four-player free-for-all.

- A clear notice identifies the bots before you accept. You can search for real players instead.
- Bot wins earn ranked points. Losses cost points, with the usual lower-rank protection, lifetime peaks and separate format ladders.
- In 2v2, your teammate and both opponents are identified as bots. The bots follow the same rules and selected clock as you.
- Each introduction counts when it starts. Reconnecting, abandoning a game, deranking or a new season cannot refill the allowance. Previous ranked matches count toward the first three matches in that format.
- Bots are virtual seats, not accounts, and never appear on leaderboards or online-player lists.
- Resigning or timing out ends a bot introduction promptly and records one result. Human team and free-for-all games retain their normal continuation rules.

The playable tutorial, friends, unique usernames, achievements, four leaderboards, five clocks, CC0 sounds, offline saves and Android updater are retained. Existing saved games and accounts carry forward. Seasons remain inactive.

This is the direct Android beta update; installing an update still needs Android's approval. The unsigned classic Windows build remains private. A separate Google Play edition is being prepared with the approved one-time R10 price and no ads; this release is not a claim of Play Store availability.
